Wayne Engineering Society - Giving Guide

The Wayne Engineering Society (formerly Theta Tau EB Chapter) has updated their giving opportunities for both direct (non-deductible) donations and tax-deductible donations.

The tax-deductible Endowment Fund is dedicated to Wayne Engineering Society (“WES”) full-time undergraduate and graduate WSU Engineering students and assists with tuition, fees, and housing costs - provided that the student lives in the WES House. Further, this new Fund is far better than the previous professional engineering Fund as it provides visibility into the Fund balance, scholarship amounts available and allows the WES Alumni Association to set some criteria for scholarships.

Direct Donations (non-tax deductible)

The process for regular Alumni donations has not changed. The donations are highly appreciated and are directed primarily towards the operating costs of the House. You can donate online, or via check.

Online: Through GoFundMe (credit card accepted) at https://gofund.me/90124590

Or mail a check payable to “Epsilon Beta Alumni Association” to 32557 Sabrina Ct., Warren, MI 48093-8142

Tax-Deductible Giving (Online)

Online: (at WSU website: https://give.wayne.edu/campaigns/37578/donations/new
Under the Designation Box, scroll down to “Other” and type in “Wayne Engineering Society Endowed Scholarship” – all of the other boxes please fill in as stated.

IMPORTANT: The proper Fund must be defined, otherwise any donated monies will go into the general fund over which we have no control. The proper fund is:

Wayne Engineering Society Endowed Scholarship

You may specify either:

  1. 'Endowment' which is the principal amount from which financial returns will be available for scholarships perpetually, or
  2. 'Spendable' which means your donated funds will be used immediately for scholarships.

We suggest you donate to 'Endowment' as this provides funds in perpetuity.

Tax-Deductible Giving (Mail-in)

If you prefer to donate via a paper check, the mailing address is: 

Wayne State University Gift Processing
5700 Cass Avenue, Suite 1200
Detroit, MI 48202
With the check made out to:
"Wayne State University" with "Wayne Engineering Society Endowed Scholarship" noted on the memo line.

Tax-Deductible Verification

Wayne State University will send you a confirmation letter for your donation. Please verify that the letter confirms a donation to the “Wayne Engineering Society”. This is important as we have had a couple instances where donations have gone into the General Fund because the clerk did not read the memo line on the check.

We further ask that you send an email to wesdonations@fastmail.com notifying the WES Alumni Association of your gift (we do not need the amount, just your name) so that it can be reconciled against the annual statement that the WES Alumni Association receives from Wayne State University.

Other Matters / Support for Tax-Deductible Donations

Please contact Kris Theisen if you have further questions at (734) 735-8218 or KrisTheisen@wayne.edu. Kris should also be contacted if your donation confirmation letter from Wayne State University does not include "Wayne Engineering Society" in the Designation column.

For Bequeaths, Wills and Trusts

For those of you who have included the former Theta Tau entities as a beneficiary in your Estate Plan, Will or Trust, we encourage you to immediately replace that language with:
"Wayne Engineering Society Endowed Scholarship in the Anderson College of Engineering at Wayne State University, Detroit, Michigan"
