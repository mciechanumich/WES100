WAYNE ENGINEERING SOCIETY

WES 100th Anniversary Event

The original engineering society, Epsilon Sigma, was founded on December 12, 1926. This year marks the 100th anniversary of the founding. The new name is Wayne Engineering Society (WES).

Alumni and student members will celebrate the 100-year anniversary on Friday, October 9, 2026, 5pm to 8pm. The usual monthly Friday poker will take place immediately after, from 8pm to 10pm.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cost of the event?

A donation of $30 to cover the food. There are additional optional donation tiers.

What kind of food will be at the event?

We are arranging to have heavy hours d'oeuvres in a strolling dinner setting.

What will be on the tour?

We will have a walking self-guided tour of recent house improvements including the front fence, the electrical, the kitchen renovation, the third-floor shower and bathroom, and the boiler room. Each tour stop will have web app info describing the history and story behind the project.

What are donation tiers?

Century Club is $100. Gold tier is (TBD) and includes a early guided tour of the recent house improvements.

How can alumni donate?

We will include information about donating on the registration site.

How do I register for the event?

We will set up the alumni GoFundMe site for alumni to register for the event. Details coming.

Who is planning this event?

The planning committee has members from the EB Alumni Association, the Housing Corp., the Endowment Fund, and the WES Student Members.

Can alumni stay for poker/games afterwards?

Yes. We encourage alumni to join the monthly poker 8pm to 10pm. (Yes, we know this is the SECOND Friday instead of the FIRST Friday. We reschedule the First-Friday poker just this once). If you want to organize and host other games, please let us know so we can reserve space. Bringing your own folding table and plan to some during set up.

We are working on arranging a Zoom call to take place during the event. Virtual attendees will have access to the slideshow and a virtual tour of the recent house renovations. Virtual attendees will still need to register to attend. Details to come.

How do I get my photos into the slideshow?

We will have a running slideshow showing photos from the past 100 years. To submit photos, alumni should contact Mark C. or Bryan.

What is the attire for the event?

Casual, or 1920's attire to celebrate the founding in 1926. Activities will include a strolling dinner and house self-tour. Cards and games

We need many items and much voluteer help to make this event a success. We are seeking photos from the past to include in a running slideshow. We will have a volunteer sign up app. Set up will take 3 to 4 hours before the event. Details coming soon.
