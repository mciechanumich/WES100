WES 100th Anniversary Event

The original engineering society, Epsilon Sigma, was founded on December 12, 1926. This year marks the 100th anniversary of the founding.

Alumni and student members will celebrate the 100-year anniversary on Friday, October 9, 2026, 5pm to 8pm. The usual monthly Friday poker will take place immediately after, from 8pm to 10pm.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cost of the event?

A donation of $30 to cover the food. There are additional optional donation tiers.

What kind of food will be at the event?

We are arranging to have heavy hours d'oeuvres in a walking dinner setting.

What will be on tour?

We will have a walking self-guided tour of recent house improvements including the front fence, the electrical, the kitchen renovation, the third-floor bathroon, and the boiler room. Each tour stop will have a web app info describing the history and story behind the improvement.

What are donation tiers?

Century Club is $100. Gold tier is (TBD) and includes a early guided tour of the recent house improvements.

How can alumni donate?

We will include information about donating on the registration site.

How do I register for the event?

We will set up the alumni GoFundMe site for alumni to register for the event. Details coming.

Who is planning this event?

The planning committee has members from the EB Alumni Association, the Housing Corp., the Endowment Fund, and the WES Student Members.

Can alumni stay for poker/games afterwards?

Yes. We encourage alumni to join monthly poker or bring other games.

What are the virtual attendance options?

We plan to set up Zoom call to take place during the event.

How do I get my photos into the slideshow?

We will have a running slideshow showing photos from the past 100 years. To submit photos, alumni should contact Mark C. or Bryan.

How can alumni volunteer to bring items or help out at the event?

We need many items and much voluteer help to make this event a success. Alumni can volunteer to help out for an hour during the event, help set up, clean up afterward, or help by bringing needed items. We are seeking photos from the past to include in a running slideshow. We will have a volunteer sign up app. Details coming soon.
